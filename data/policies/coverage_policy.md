# Coverage Policy

## COV-01 Annual Limit
Each insured member has an annual coverage limit of PKR 1,000,000 per policy year. Claims above the remaining annual balance are partially payable up to the balance.

## COV-02 Inpatient Treatment
Hospital admissions of at least 24 hours are covered, including room charges, surgery, medicines, lab tests and consultant fees. Standard room rent is covered up to PKR 15,000 per day. Private room charges above this limit are paid by the patient.

## COV-03 Outpatient Treatment
Outpatient (OPD) consultations, prescribed medicines and lab tests are covered up to PKR 50,000 per policy year.

## COV-04 Maternity
Normal delivery is covered up to PKR 80,000 and C-section up to PKR 150,000. Maternity benefits apply only after a 9 month waiting period from the policy start date.

## COV-05 Emergency Care
Emergency treatment at any hospital is covered, including ambulance charges up to PKR 10,000 per event.

## COV-06 Required Documents
Every claim must include the hospital bill, discharge summary (for admissions), doctor prescription, and lab or imaging reports where applicable. Claims without required documents are marked as needing review.
