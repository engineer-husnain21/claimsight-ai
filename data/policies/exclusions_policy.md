# Exclusions Policy

## EXC-01 Cosmetic Procedures
Cosmetic or aesthetic treatment is not covered, unless it is reconstructive surgery needed after an accident.

## EXC-02 Dental and Vision
Routine dental care, spectacles and contact lenses are not covered. Dental treatment caused by an accident is covered within 30 days of the accident.

## EXC-03 Pre-existing Conditions
Conditions diagnosed before the policy start date are not covered during the first 12 months of the policy.

## EXC-04 Experimental Treatment
Experimental, unproven or unapproved treatments and medicines are not covered.

## EXC-05 Non-medical Charges
Non-medical items such as toiletries, attendant food, telephone charges and registration fees are not covered.
