# Pre-authorization Policy

## PRE-01 Planned Admissions
Planned hospital admissions with an expected cost above PKR 100,000 require pre-authorization at least 3 days before admission.

## PRE-02 Emergency Admissions
Emergency admissions do not need pre-authorization, but the insurer must be informed within 24 hours of admission.

## PRE-03 Advanced Imaging
MRI, CT scan and PET scan require pre-authorization, except during an emergency admission.

## PRE-04 Missing Pre-authorization
Claims that required pre-authorization but do not have it are marked as needing review and may be reduced by 20 percent.
