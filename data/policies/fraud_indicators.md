# Fraud and Anomaly Indicators

## FRD-01 Duplicate Claims
The same patient, provider, service date and amount appearing in more than one claim is a strong fraud indicator.

## FRD-02 Unusual Amount
A billed amount more than double the typical cost for the procedure should be flagged for review.

## FRD-03 Date Mismatch
A service date before the policy start date, or in the future, is a fraud indicator.

## FRD-04 Diagnosis and Procedure Mismatch
A procedure that does not match the stated diagnosis (for example, knee surgery for a fever) should be flagged.

## FRD-05 High Claim Frequency
More than 3 claims from the same patient within 30 days should be flagged for review.
